About JS Dev Studio

Building Modern Web Experiences with Precision and Purpose

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit is required to secure your project and begin work. The remaining balance is due upon completion before final delivery.

How long does a project take?

Smaller updates can take 1–3 days, while full website builds typically take 1–2 weeks depending on complexity and feedback speed.

Do you work with existing websites?

Yes. I can improve, redesign, or optimize your current website, including WordPress and custom-built sites.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Absolutely. All websites are built with a mobile-first approach to ensure they look and perform perfectly on all devices.

Are revisions included?

Yes, minor revisions are included to ensure you're happy with the final result. Larger changes outside the agreed scope are quoted separately.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Ongoing support and maintenance options are available to keep your website running smoothly after launch.
